At KubeCon EU 2026, one theme kept surfacing across talks, hallway conversations, and side events: our application ambitions are moving faster than the infrastructure underneath them. 

We’re designing platforms for AI agents, autonomous workflows, and increasingly dynamic workloads, but much of the core infrastructure still reflects assumptions from a very different era. 

In this piece, I reflect on the signals I took away from Amsterdam:

*   **Platform Engineering:** How it's evolving to support more than just "standard" developer workflows.
*   **Shadow IT in GenAI:** Why developers (and agents) are bypassing official paths.
*   **Defensive Architectures:** Building resilience for autonomous agent workloads.
*   **Kubernetes Evolution:** Why the project itself is being pushed to adapt for the AI age.
